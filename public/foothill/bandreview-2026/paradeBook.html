Parade - Open

Good morning ladies and gentlemen. Welcome to the Parade Competition for the 52nd Annual Foothill High School Band Review. We are joined today by nearly three dozen wonderful bands to entertain and delight us. Foothill High School wishes good luck and good music to all the bands performing today. We hope you enjoy the competition.

Parade - Presentation of the Colors

As we start our parade, please rise if you are able, remove your caps, and give your attention and respect to our colors, presented by the color guard from Veterans of Foreign Wars Post 6298.

Parade - Grand Marshal

And now… please welcome our Grand Marshal, Pleasanton Mayor, Jack Balch. Mayor Balch is honored to serve the amazing people of Pleasanton and Foothill's Parade Competition. He is riding in a classic 2004 Porsche 911 Carrera 4S Cabriolet owned by long-time resident and Foothill graduate Jamie Yee, and driven by Ken Benhamou, owner of SAS German Auto in Pleasanton and board member of the Diablo Region Porsche Club of America .

Parade - Recurring Announcements

  • As a courtesy to the performers, spectators please step back fully onto the curb to allow the band to use the entire street.
  • As our parade continues, bands will get progressively larger.
