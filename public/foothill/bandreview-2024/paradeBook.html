50th Annual Foothill High School Band Review : Parade Announcer's Book: October 26, 2024

Eric Berdahl <berdahl@serendipity.org>

v0.01, October 25, 2024 at 8:06 PM PDT

Parade - Open

Good morning ladies and gentlemen. Welcome to the Parade Competition for the 50th Annual Foothill High School Band Review. We are joined today by nearly two score wonderful bands to entertain and delight us. Foothill High School wishes good luck and good music to all the bands performing today. We hope you enjoy the competition.

Parade - Presentation of the Colors

As we start our parade, please rise, remove your caps, and give your attention and respect to our colors, presented by the color guard from Veterans of Foreign Wars Post 6298.

Parade - Grand Marshal

And now… please welcome our Grand Marshal, Foothill High School's first Director of Bands and the person who launched the Foothill Band Review 49 years ago, Bob Moorefield.

Parade - Recurring Announcements

  • As a courtesy to the performers, spectators please step back fully onto the curb to allow the band to use the entire street.
  • As our parade continues, bands will get progressively larger.

This concludes the Parade Competition for the 50th Annual Foothill High School Band Review. Thank you and congratulations to all the bands we've seen this morning, and thank you all for attending and cheering on these talented young performers.

Parade Awards will be presented at 2:00pm in the Amador Valley High School Stadium.

Please join us at the Foothill High School Stadium for the Field Show Competition, starting at 4:00pm this afternoon and running through this evening.

Mark your calendars to join us again next year, on October 24, 2024, for the 51st Annual Foothill High School Band Review. On behalf of Foothill High School, I wish you all a safe and enjoyable weekend. Good day.
